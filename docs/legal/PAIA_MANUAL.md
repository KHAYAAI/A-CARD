## 7. How to make a request
1. Complete **Form 2** (request for access to a record of a private body), available from the Information Regulator, and send it to the Information Officer at privacy@a-card.cc.
2. Give enough detail to identify the record, the form of access you want, and the right you're exercising or protecting.
3. Pay the request fee and any access fee prescribed in the PAIA regulations. We'll tell you the amount.
4. We respond within **30 days** (extendable once by 30 days with notice).
Requests by data subjects for their own personal information can also be made under POPIA s23, free of charge for confirmation of whether we hold it.

## 8. Grounds for refusal
We may refuse access on the grounds in PAIA Chapter 4 of Part 3, including protecting third parties' personal information (s63), commercial information (s64), confidential information (s65), safety of individuals and property (s66), legal privilege (s67), and our commercial and research information (s68).

## 9. Remedies
If refused, you may complain to the Information Regulator within 180 days or apply to court.
