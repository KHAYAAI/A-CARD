# A-CARD Privacy Policy

**DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW.** Effective 1 February 2027.

Agent Card (Pty) Ltd ("A-CARD") is the **responsible party** for the personal information described here, under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). Information Officer: [INFORMATION OFFICER], privacy@a-card.cc, 28 Grenville Avenue, Savoy Estate, Johannesburg, 2090.

## 1. What we collect
| Category | What | From |
|---|---|---|
| Account | name, email, phone, company, password (stored only as a hash), passkey public keys | you |
| Verification | ID document details, photo, face liveness (biometric), proof of address, company registration and owners, sanctions/PEP results | you, via Didit |
| Payment | card brand, last 4 digits, expiry month/year, vault reference. **Never the full card number or security code**, which go straight to Basis Theory | you, Basis Theory |
| Wallets | wallet addresses, balances, transfers, screening results, Travel Rule names | platform, VALR, Didit |
| Agent activity | instructions, Policy decisions, approvals, purchases, merchants | platform |
| Billing | plan, invoices, payment status | PayFast |
| Technical | IP address, browser, device, logs, cookies needed to stay signed in | automatic |

We do not knowingly process information of children under 18.

## 2. Why, and on what basis (POPIA s11)
| Purpose | Basis |
|---|---|
| Run your account, Agents, cards and wallets | Contract with you |
| Verify identity, prevent fraud and money laundering, screen sanctions, Travel Rule | Legal obligation (FICA and directives) |
| Biometric liveness check | Your consent (s27(1)(a)) [⚖️ Counsel] |
| Security, abuse prevention, service improvement | Legitimate interest |
| Product news | Consent; opt out any time (s69) |

We do not sell personal information. We do not use it to make automated decisions with legal effect without a human able to review them, except fraud and sanctions blocks required by law, which you may ask us to review (s71).

## 3. Who we share with
Our operators (service providers) under written agreements: see `SUBPROCESSORS.md`. Also: merchants you buy from (what's needed to fulfil the order); our partners for live financial services (Basis Theory, VALR, [card issuer]); regulators, the FIC, SARS and law enforcement where the law requires; professional advisers; a buyer of our business, under the same protections.

## 4. Transfers outside South Africa (s72)
Some operators process data outside South Africa (e.g. US, EU). We transfer only where the recipient is bound by law, binding corporate rules or an agreement giving protection substantially similar to POPIA, or where you consent or it's needed for our contract with you.

## 5. How long we keep it
| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Verification and transaction records | 5 years after the relationship ends (FICA s22–23) |
| Account and Agent activity | life of the account + 5 years |
| Biometric liveness data | as held by Didit per its retention, no longer than needed to verify you [⚖️ Counsel] |
| Logs | 12 months |
| Marketing preferences | until you withdraw |
Then we delete or de-identify it.

## 6. Security (s19)
Encryption in transit and at rest; sensitive references encrypted at field level; wallet keys held in AWS KMS hardware; card data held only by a PCI DSS Level 1 vault; passkeys; least-privilege access; monitoring; breach procedure. See `INFORMATION_SECURITY_POLICY.md`.

## 7. Your rights
You may ask to access, correct, or delete your information, object to processing, withdraw consent, and opt out of marketing (s23–25, s11(3), s69). Email privacy@a-card.cc; we reply within 30 days. Some data must be kept by law (section 5). You may complain to the **Information Regulator**: inforeg.org.za, complaints.IR@inforegulator.org.za. Access requests may also be made under PAIA; see our PAIA manual.
