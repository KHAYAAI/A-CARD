# Agent Authority Terms

**DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW.** Part of the A-CARD Terms of Service.

## 1. The mandate
1.1 When you create an Agent and give it a key, card or wallet, you authorise A-CARD to carry out payment instructions from that Agent on your behalf, **only within the Policies you set**.
1.2 A Policy may include: amount per transaction, per day, per month; allowed or blocked merchants and categories; allowed countries and currencies; time windows; whether a human approval (passkey) is needed, and above what amount.
1.3 An instruction from your Agent within your Policies is attributed to you (ECTA s25(b)), whether or not you saw it first.

## 2. What we do
2.1 Check every instruction against your Policies before acting, and refuse anything outside them.
2.2 Record each instruction, the Policy decision, and any approval, so you can review them.
2.3 Let you pause or revoke an Agent, key or card at any time, effective immediately for new instructions.

## 3. What you do
3.1 Set Policies that match the risk you accept. Review Agent activity regularly.
3.2 Keep Agent keys secret and revoke them if exposed.
3.3 Turn on human approval for anything you would not want to happen without you.
3.4 Understand that Agents can be wrong, and can be misled by content they read (for example "prompt injection" on a web page). Policies are the limit of your exposure; set them accordingly.

## 4. Who bears what
| Situation | Who bears the loss |
|---|---|
| Agent buys within your Policies, even if it was a poor choice | You (claim against the merchant if the goods are faulty) |
| A-CARD lets through a transaction outside your Policies | A-CARD refunds the excess (Terms 9.4) |
| Your Agent key is stolen and used within Policies before you revoke it | You, unless we were negligent; we limit exposure to your Policies |
| A transaction after you revoked the Agent or key | A-CARD |
| A merchant fails to deliver | Merchant; we help with the dispute |
| Card charge you didn't authorise | Your card issuer's dispute process applies; we provide our records |

## 5. Approvals
A passkey approval confirms you authorised that specific charge, for that amount and merchant. Recovering access with recovery codes pauses approvals and limit increases for 24 hours as a safety measure.
