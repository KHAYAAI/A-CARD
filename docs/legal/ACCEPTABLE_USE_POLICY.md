# Acceptable Use Policy

**DRAFT FOR COUNSEL REVIEW.** Part of the Terms of Service. Applies to you, your Agents and (for merchants) what you sell.

You must not use A-CARD, or let an Agent use it, to:
1. break any law, including on money laundering, terrorist financing, sanctions, fraud, tax evasion or gambling;
2. buy or sell: illegal drugs or drug paraphernalia; weapons, ammunition or explosives; stolen goods; counterfeit or infringing goods; adult content; unlicensed gambling, lotteries or betting; unlicensed financial products, securities or crypto schemes; human trafficking or exploitation; anything sanctioned by South Africa, the UN, the EU, the UK, the UAE or the US OFAC lists;
3. send to or receive from sanctioned people, countries or wallets, or mixers and tumblers designed to hide where funds came from;
4. structure transactions to avoid limits, screening or reporting;
5. impersonate anyone, or open accounts for others without authority;
6. probe, overload, or break our security, or reverse engineer the service, except under our security reporting policy;
7. use stolen cards or cards you're not authorised to use;
8. run Agents with no limits on behalf of other people without their informed authorisation;
9. resell A-CARD without our written agreement.

We may refuse transactions, suspend Agents or accounts, and report to authorities where the law requires. Report abuse to support@a-card.cc (security issues: security@a-card.cc).
