A‑CARD: who we compete with, and where each one really stands

Status as reported in public sources, November 2026. Most sources are secondary (news, blogs, trackers) and some conflict; verify against each company's own announcements before quoting.

What A‑CARD is: a control layer that lets a company's AI agents spend money safely. Each agent gets its own virtual card or wallet with limits, merchant rules and human approvals; it finds suppliers (A‑MERCHANT); each payment is routed over card, x402 or stablecoin (AFP); one ledger and audit log. Rand‑first. Today: a working sandbox on test money, not live.

Where A‑CARD is differentRand‑first; one product for supplier discovery, controlled cards, wallets and rail routing; humans stay in control (approvals, policy, audit); works end to end in a sandbox today.
Where we're weakerNot live; no issuing partner, licences or SOC 2 yet. Competitors are shipping to real customers, and the networks and Stripe have far more resources.
